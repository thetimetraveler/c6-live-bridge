    Rooms

    Each room compares three things: what Lutron shows, what the bridge last sent, and what the lamp reports. Lutron is the reference; when a lamp differs, set the level from Lutron again. In sync means within 2 percentage points of brightness and 10 mired of colour.

    Open a room for its details. On the bar, the fill is the lamp, ▼ is Lutron, the thin line is what the bridge sent and ◆ is the show target. K is colour temperature (lower is warmer); vibrancy is Ketra's colour saturation, 0–100.

    Readings count as current for 2 minutes through the N4 hub and 7 minutes on direct Zigbee, which reads each lamp after a command and every 5 minutes (every 30 s during a Natural Show).
